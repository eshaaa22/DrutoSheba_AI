DrutoSheba AI

One click. Ambulance on the way. Bed ready.

An AI-based emergency response system that connects patients, ambulances, and hospitals in Bangladesh.

Try the live demoRead the idea

The Problem

In an emergency, every minute matters. Patients lose time, money, and sometimes life because no single, fair system connects the patient, the ambulance, and the hospital.

No bed information

Families cannot know which emergency ward has a free bed or an available doctor.

Hospital to hospital

Patients lose time and money moving from one hospital to another to find a bed.

Slow ambulance, wrong address

Finding an ambulance takes time, and the driver may not find the exact address quickly.

Late preparation

The hospital learns about the patient only on arrival, so preparation starts late.

Narrow roads

In many areas, narrow lanes stop the ambulance from reaching the patient's door.

Unequal access

There are public concerns that influence can decide who gets a bed first.

Many hospitals are also not registered on any online system, so no shared and reliable information exists.

Our Solution

One system that sends the ambulance, chooses a suitable hospital, and prepares the bed before the patient arrives.

1. Press

One tap or quick shortcut. Location is shared.

2. Ambulance

AI matches the nearest free ambulance.

3. Hospital ready

Bed reserved. Ward and doctor alerted before arrival.

4. Treatment

Straight to the ready bed. The doctor decides on any operation.

Objectives

  1. Provide a one-click and quick-shortcut emergency alert that is easy to use under stress.
  2. Send the nearest available ambulance to the exact location.
  3. Guide patients to a suitable hospital so they do not move from place to place for a bed.
  4. Reserve an emergency bed and alert the ward and doctor before arrival.
  5. Support fair bed allocation based on medical urgency, with a clear record.
  6. Solve the last stretch problem in narrow roads through pickup points and volunteers.
  7. Use AI to improve ambulance and hospital selection.
  8. Keep users active with daily-use features, and grow step by step with more hospitals and ambulance providers.

Innovation

What is new: not only an ambulance call, but the missing link of a ready bed and fair access.

Quick trigger

Works without opening the app: widget, button pattern, voice, SMS.

Bed reservation

The hospital is alerted in advance, so the bed, ward team, and doctor are ready.

Fair bed ranking

Ranked by medical urgency and arrival time. Every decision is recorded, and changes need a written reason.

Narrow road help

Pickup points and nearby volunteers cover the last stretch.

Starts without registration

Phase 1 works even if hospitals are not online yet.

Daily-use features

Medicine reminders and blood donation keep the app on the phone.

AI is used for ambulance matching, hospital recommendation, ward workload prediction, and urgency support. AI supports the decision and never replaces the doctor.

      Alert System

      Fast, simple under panic, and reliable with weak internet.

      Ways to send an alert

      Home or lock screen widget, quick settings tile (Android), button pattern, voice command, smartwatch or Bluetooth button, and SMS or call fallback. Button access differs by phone platform and must be checked for each.

      Preventing accidental alerts

      Countdown with cancel, a deliberate press pattern, a quick check, phone verification with a misuse record, and a confirmation call for unclear cases.

      Who is alerted

      The nearest ambulance, the selected hospital, family contacts by SMS, and nearby volunteers.

      Weak internet

      Alerts can go by SMS. A phone call is the last fallback, and the last known location is sent if live location is missing.

      Expected Impact

      These are expected impacts. They will be measured in a pilot, for example the time from emergency call to a ready bed and the number of hospital changes per patient.

      Social

      Faster treatment, equal access to care, support for elderly people, and easier blood donation.

      Economic

      Lower travel cost and effort for families, and better use of ambulances, beds, and doctors.

      Environmental

      Shorter routes and fewer unnecessary trips between hospitals mean less wasted fuel and traffic.

      Roadmap and Challenges

      The first release focuses on the emergency alert. Daily-use features follow step by step.

      Now

      Emergency alert, ambulance matching, hospital alert, Medical ID, family SMS

      Next

      Medicine reminder with medicine photo, family safety circle, appointment reminders

      Then

      Blood donation network, volunteer responders, pregnancy mode, offline first-aid guide

      Later

      Health records, telemedicine, wearable support

      Challenges and our response

      ChallengeOur response
      Hospitals not registeredStart with ambulance and hospital lists; add partner hospitals gradually.
      Wrong or old bed dataShow last update time, ask hospitals to confirm, never show uncertain data as fact.
      Influence and unfair priorityUrgency-based ranking, a record of decisions, written reasons for changes. Technology alone cannot remove influence, so hospital cooperation is needed.
      Narrow roadsPickup points, volunteer helpers, smaller vehicles.
      Medical responsibilityThe system only prepares the hospital. The doctor decides.
      Accidental or false alertsCountdown with cancel, press pattern, verification, misuse record.
      Privacy of patient dataCollect only what is needed, ask for consent, encrypt data.
      Weak internetSMS and call fallback.
      Cost and legal rulesPlan partnerships and check health and data regulations.
